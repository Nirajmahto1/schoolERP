**Between:** [Your legal name] ("Provider") and [School trust/society name],
[address] ("School") · Effective: [date]

## 1. Pilot scope & duration
- Product: EduCore ERP, modules: [list], branches covered: [N]
- Pilot term: [90] days from go-live, extendable once by mutual written agreement
- Fees: waived / [X]% of list price during the pilot; onboarding fee [waived / ₹___]

## 2. Feedback obligations (the point of a pilot)
- School nominates the champion as single point of contact
- One 30-minute feedback call per fortnight during the pilot
- Participation in a written case study if outcomes are positive, with
  factual numbers, subject to School's written approval of the text

## 3. Data
- School's data remains School's property; exportable at any time (§13.5.3)
- Provider processes data only to deliver the service; children's data handled
  per the DPDP Act 2023 (consent registry, erasure path, breach runbook)
- On pilot exit: full data export provided within 10 business days; Provider
  deletes School's data within 30 days of written confirmation

## 4. Success criteria (converts the pilot into a decision)
- ≥ [70]% of parents using the parent app within 30 days of rollout
- Attendance marked digitally on ≥ [90]% of school days
- Fee collection records fully digital from go-live
- Review meeting at day [75]: proceed to subscription, extend, or wind down

## 5. Support & uptime
- WhatsApp support line during school hours; critical issues [4]h response
- Uptime commitment: [99.5]% monthly, measured per docs/ops/RUNBOOKS.md
- Provider access to School data only via time-boxed, audited support grants
  with reasons visible to School

## 6. Liability & confidentiality
- Mutual confidentiality; Provider liability capped at fees paid (skeleton —
  lawyer to advise on caps, indemnities, insurance for children's data)
- Either party may terminate the pilot with [14] days' written notice
