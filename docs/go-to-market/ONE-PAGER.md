## The problem you know too well

- **Fees leak.** Cash registers, UPI on personal phones, Excel registers that
  nobody reconciles — and every year, lakhs go missing in the gaps.
- **Parents find out late.** An absence is discovered at 6 pm, not at 9 am.
  A fee reminder is a Wednesday phone-call ritual.
- **Excel runs the school.** Timetables, report cards, and comparison across
  campuses all live in one coordinator's spreadsheet — and that person's leave
  stops the school.

## What EduCore does on day one

| Your staff does this | EduCore does this |
|---|---|
| Teacher marks attendance | Parent's phone buzzes within seconds — by name |
| Office collects a fee (cash or UPI) | Numbered receipt, ledger entry, and the parent's app updates — one trail |
| Term-end marks entry | CBSE-format report cards published to parents, zero formatting |
| Coordinator builds a timetable | Clash detection catches the double-booked teacher before students do |
| Principal asks "how are we doing?" | Dashboard answers: enrollment, collection rate, attendance %, per campus |

## Built for how Indian schools actually run

- **Multi-branch groups**: one login for the owner, per-campus dashboards,
  consolidated fee and attendance numbers.
- **Razorpay fee collection** with automatic reconciliation — every online
  rupee matches a receipt, every mismatch is flagged.
- **Late fees that are fair**: your rules, your slabs, applied nightly,
  reversed in one click with an audit trail.
- **The parent app** (Android): fees, receipts, attendance, results, class
  chat, school announcements, push notifications — offline-tolerant.
- **DPDP-compliant from day one**: consent registry, data export, erasure,
  breach runbook — procurement-ready for 2026.
- **Your data is yours**: isolated per-school database, exportable any time.
